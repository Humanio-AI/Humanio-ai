How do I submit an expense claim on SAP Concur?

To submit an expense claim in SAP Concur:

Log in to SAP Concur using your company credentials.

From the homepage, select Expense.

Click Create New Report and enter the required report details (report name, business purpose, and dates).

Add expenses by either:

Uploading receipts (photo, PDF, or email-forwarded receipts), or

Manually creating an expense and entering the details.

Select the correct expense category for each item.

Ensure all required fields are completed and receipts are attached where required.

Review the report for accuracy.

Submit the report for approval.

Once submitted, the report will route automatically to your manager or the relevant approver.

What expenses are reimbursable and what are not?

Reimbursable expenses typically include:

Business travel (train, flight, taxi, ride-share)

Mileage for approved business travel

Accommodation for business trips

Client or business meals (within policy limits)

Office supplies required for work

Professional subscriptions or training (if approved)

Non-reimbursable expenses typically include:

Personal meals or entertainment

Fines, penalties, or parking tickets

Alcohol (unless explicitly approved as part of client entertainment)

Personal travel or accommodation

Home internet, utilities, or rent (unless contractually agreed)

Meals when working from home (see section below)

Always refer to the company expense policy for full details.

When are expenses paid?

Approved expense claims are usually paid:

In the next scheduled payment run after final approval

Typically within 5–10 working days of approval

Payment timing may vary depending on payroll cut-off dates and bank processing times. Expenses are paid directly to the bank account registered in SAP Concur.

Who do I contact if I have a payroll or payment issue?

If you experience an issue with payroll or expense payments:

First, check the status of your expense or payslip in SAP Concur or the payroll system.

If the issue is unresolved, contact the Finance or Payroll team via:

Email: finance@company.com (example)

Internal ticketing system or HR portal (if applicable)

Please include relevant details such as employee ID, report number, and dates to help resolve the issue quickly.

What is the mileage reimbursement rate?

Mileage is reimbursed at the company-approved rate for business travel using a personal vehicle.

Standard mileage rate: £0.45 per mile for the first 10,000 miles per tax year

Reduced rate: £0.25 per mile after 10,000 miles

Mileage claims must:

Be for genuine business travel

Include start and end locations

Exclude normal commuting

What is the approval limit for managers?

Manager approval limits vary by role and seniority.

Typical guidelines:

Line managers: up to £1,000 per expense report

Senior managers or department heads: higher limits as defined by Finance

Expenses above the approval limit may require secondary approval

Approval limits are enforced automatically within SAP Concur.


-----

When is payroll processing cut-off each month?

Payroll cut-off is typically:

Between the 20th and 25th of each month

All changes affecting payroll (expenses, bank details, salary changes, allowances) must be submitted and approved before the cut-off date to be included in that month’s payroll.

Late submissions will be processed in the following payroll cycle.


-----

How do I correct a rejected expense claim on SAP Concur?

If your expense claim is rejected:

Open the rejected report in SAP Concur.

Review the rejection comments provided by the approver.

Make the required corrections (e.g. update details, attach missing receipts, change expense category).

Save the changes.

Re-submit the report for approval.

Do not create a new report unless instructed to do so by Finance.


-----

Are meals reimbursed when working from home?

Meals are generally not reimbursed when working from home, as they are considered personal expenses.

Exceptions may apply if:

You are required to work extended hours by management

The meal is part of an approved business event or meeting

There is prior written approval from Finance or management

If unsure, seek approval before submitting the expense.
