HR POLICIES, BENEFITS & PEOPLE PROCESSES
=======================================

This document provides detailed guidance on employee and manager HR policies, benefits, and processes. It is intended to support self-service access to accurate and consistent HR information.

--------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT IS THE MATERNITY POLICY?
--------------------------------------------------

The organisation supports employees through pregnancy, childbirth, and return to work in line with statutory requirements.

Maternity Leave Entitlement:
- Employees are entitled to up to 52 weeks of maternity leave, regardless of length of service.
  - Ordinary Maternity Leave (OML): First 26 weeks
  - Additional Maternity Leave (AML): Further 26 weeks

Maternity Pay:
- Eligible employees may receive Statutory Maternity Pay (SMP).
- SMP is paid as follows:
  - First 6 weeks: 90% of average weekly earnings (before tax)
  - Remaining eligible weeks: Paid at the statutory weekly rate
- Eligibility depends on length of service and earnings threshold.

Notification Requirements:
- Employees must notify the company at least 15 weeks before the expected week of childbirth.
- A MATB1 certificate must be provided.
- Employees should confirm:
  - Intended start date of maternity leave
  - Expected week of childbirth

During Maternity Leave:
- Contract of employment remains in place.
- Annual leave continues to accrue.
- Pension contributions continue according to scheme rules.
- Employees may take up to 10 Keeping in Touch (KIT) days without ending maternity leave.

Returning to Work:
- Employees returning within 26 weeks are entitled to the same role.
- Employees returning after 26 weeks are entitled to the same or a suitable alternative role.
- Flexible working requests can be submitted on return.

--------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT IS THE PATERNITY POLICY?
--------------------------------------------------

The organisation provides statutory paternity leave to eligible employees.

Paternity Leave Entitlement:
- Up to 2 weeks of paternity leave.
- Leave must be taken as one continuous block.

Pay:
- Statutory Paternity Pay (SPP) is paid where eligibility criteria are met.
- Paid at the statutory weekly rate or 90% of average weekly earnings (whichever is lower).

Eligibility:
- Employee must be:
  - The child’s father
  - The partner of the mother
  - The intended parent (including adoption or surrogacy)
- Must have at least 26 weeks of continuous service by the qualifying week.
- Must provide notice at least 15 weeks before the expected week of birth.

Timing:
- Leave must be taken within 56 days of the birth or placement.

3. WHAT BENEFITS AM I ENTITLED TO?
--------------------------------------------------

Employees are entitled to a range of contractual and statutory benefits.

Core Benefits:
- Annual leave entitlement
- Pension scheme with employer contributions
- Statutory sick pay
- Family-related leave (maternity, paternity, parental leave)

Additional Benefits (subject to role and eligibility):
- Private medical insurance
- Life assurance
- Income protection
- Employee Assistance Programme (EAP)
- Flexible working arrangements
- Learning and development funding

Viewing Benefits:
- Employees can view benefit details in Workday:
  Profile > Pay & Benefits
- Benefit enrolment and changes may be subject to annual windows.

4. HOW DO I BOOK TRAINING ON THE LMS?
--------------------------------------------------

Training and development courses are managed through the Learning Management System (LMS) within Workday.

Steps to Book Training:
1. Log into Workday.
2. Select the Learning or LMS application.
3. Search for the required course using keywords or categories.
4. Select the course to view objectives, duration, and delivery method.
5. Click “Enroll” or “Request Enrollment”.

Approvals:
- Some courses require manager approval.
- Notifications are sent once approval is granted.

After Enrolment:
- Courses appear in your learning dashboard.
- Progress and completion are tracked automatically.
- Certificates are issued where applicable.

5. HOW DO I PROCESS A 1:1 (ONE-TO-ONE) ON WORKDAY?
--------------------------------------------------

Managers are encouraged to hold regular 1:1 meetings to support performance, engagement, and development.

Recording a 1:1 in Workday:
1. Log into Workday.
2. Navigate to the employee’s profile.
3. Select “Performance” or “Check-ins”.
4. Create a new check-in or 1:1 record.
5. Add:
   - Discussion topics
   - Feedback
   - Development actions
   - Follow-up items
6. Save or submit the record.

Purpose of 1:1s:
- Review performance and objectives
- Discuss wellbeing and engagement
- Capture development needs
- Track agreed actions

6. I NEED TO RECRUIT SOMEBODY – WHAT IS THE PROCESS?
--------------------------------------------------

All recruitment activity is managed through Workday Recruiting.

Recruitment Process:
1. Identify the need for a new or replacement role.
2. Create a job requisition in Workday.
3. Complete required details:
   - Job title and description
   - Location and working pattern
   - Salary range and justification
4. Submit the requisition for approval.
5. Once approved, the role is advertised.
6. Review applications and shortlist candidates.
7. Conduct interviews and assessments.
8. Select preferred candidate.
9. Submit offer details in Workday.
10. On acceptance, onboarding is initiated.

HR Support:
- HR will support approvals, compliance, and onboarding.

--------------------------------------------------
7. HOW MUCH ANNUAL LEAVE AM I ENTITLED TO?
--------------------------------------------------

Annual Leave Entitlement:
- Full-time employees are entitled to 25 days of annual leave per year.
- This is in addition to public or bank holidays.

Part-Time Employees:
- Leave is pro-rated based on contracted hours.

Accrual:
- Annual leave accrues monthly.
- Entitlement may vary in the first year of employment.

Booking Leave:
- All leave requests must be submitted via Workday:
  Profile > Absence > Request Time Off
- Leave is subject to manager approval.

Unused Leave:
- Carry-over rules apply and are defined in the annual leave policy.
